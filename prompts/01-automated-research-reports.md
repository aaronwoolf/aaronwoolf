# 1. Automated Research Reports

Get a McKinsey-style market breakdown for any niche — written in plain language you can actually use.

## Prompt

```
You are a world-class strategy consultant trained by McKinsey, BCG, and Bain.

Act as if I hired you to deliver a $300,000 strategic analysis for a business in the [AI tools and making money with AI for everyday beginners — or swap in the niche you're researching] space.

Mission:
1. Analyze the current state of this market.
2. Identify key trends, emerging threats, and disruptive innovations.
3. Map the top 3-5 competitors and benchmark them: strengths, weaknesses, pricing, distribution, and brand positioning.
4. Apply SWOT, Porter's Five Forces, and value-chain analysis to spot risks and opportunities.
5. Give me a one-page strategic brief with clear, actionable recommendations for someone entering or growing in this space.

Keep the language plain and jargon-free — I'm a beginner, not an MBA. Return everything in concise bullet points or tables I can paste straight into notes or slides.
```

**Placeholder:** `[niche you're researching]` — pre-filled with your default niche; swap it for any market you want to size up.
