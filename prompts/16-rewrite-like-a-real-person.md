# 16. Rewrite It Like a Real Person

Strip the stiff, written-to-impress feel out of a draft.

```

Rewrite this the way I'd actually say it out loud: casual, bold, direct, and human — a little rougher, more natural, no corporate jargon. Cut anything that feels rehearsed, exaggerated, or written to impress. Make it sound authentic and genuinely human, never salesy or AI-generated.
