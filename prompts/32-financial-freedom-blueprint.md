# 32. Financial Freedom Blueprint

A realistic, jargon-free money plan — budget, debt payoff, savings, and ways to earn more.

## Prompt

```
My current money situation: [income, expenses, debt, savings]. My goals: [short-term and long-term]. Create a personalized financial plan with: a realistic monthly budget breakdown, a debt-payoff strategy with a timeline, a savings plan with specific percentages, 3 realistic ways to increase my income in the next 90 days, and simple investment options based on my risk tolerance and timeline. Keep it plain and beginner-friendly — no finance jargon.
```

**Placeholders:** `[income, expenses, debt, savings]` and `[goals]` — your real numbers.
