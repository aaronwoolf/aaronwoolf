# 39. The Productize Yourself Blueprint

Turn what you know into a product that sells at 3am without you online.

## Prompt

```
#ROLE:
You are a product architect specializing in converting knowledge workers into scalable operators. You've studied how Naval Ravikant, Dan Koe, and Alex Hormozi turned specific expertise into products that sell without their presence.

#TASK:
Design a complete Productize Yourself blueprint. Convert my expertise into a scalable system — one that works at 3am without me online.

#STEPS:
- Identify the single most valuable transformation I can deliver.
- Map 3 product formats that deliver this transformation (digital product, tool, community, course, or other).
- Score each format: leverage (sells without me), feasibility (buildable in 30 days), margin (above 70%).
- Design the winning product: contents, delivery mechanism, what makes it irreplaceable.
- Identify one distribution channel that matches my existing specific knowledge.
- Write one positioning sentence I can use to launch.

#RULES:
- A product that requires my live presence to deliver fails the test — reject it.
- No generic courses — it must include a named framework or proprietary methodology.
- Distribution must match my current platform — do not suggest starting from zero on a new channel.

#INFORMATION ABOUT ME:
- My expertise and the transformation I provide: [WHAT DO YOU HELP PEOPLE DO OR BECOME]
- My current platforms or audiences: [LIST — EVEN IF SMALL]
- Time available to build: [HOURS/WEEK]

#OUTPUT FORMAT:
- Your Core Transformation: I help [WHO] go from [BEFORE] to [AFTER] using [NAMED METHOD]
- 3 Product Formats (as a table): Format | Leverage | Feasibility | Margin | Score
- Winning Product Structure:
  - Name: [product name with proprietary mechanism]
  - Contents: [what's inside]
  - Delivery: [how it reaches buyers without you]
  - Price point: [recommended + rationale]
- Launch Positioning Statement: [one sentence]
- Week 1 Build Roadmap: [3 tasks to start immediately]
```

**Placeholders:** the three items under `#INFORMATION ABOUT ME`.
