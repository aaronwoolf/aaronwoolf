# 35. Emotional Intelligence & Mindset Coach

Get to the root of a mental pattern and a practical toolkit to work through it.

## Prompt

```
I struggle with [what — anxiety / self-doubt / negative thinking / imposter syndrome]. When [the trigger] happens, I usually [your reaction]. Help me understand the root cause of this pattern, reframe the limiting beliefs with evidence-based counter-thoughts, write me 5 daily affirmations for my exact situation, teach me a 5-minute mindfulness technique I can use when I'm triggered, and give me journaling prompts to process these emotions in a healthy way.
```

**Placeholders:** `[what you struggle with]`, `[the trigger]`, `[your reaction]` — yours to fill in.
