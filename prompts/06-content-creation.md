# 6. Content Creation — Newsletters, Threads, YouTube Scripts

Write content in your voice that people actually care about — human, never AI-sounding.

## Prompt

```
You are a top-tier content strategist and writer.

Write a [type of content — e.g. YouTube script, newsletter, or X/Twitter thread] on:
[your topic — e.g. "the easiest way for beginners to make money with AI"]

Guidelines:
1. Write in MY voice: casual, bold, direct, and human — plain language, no corporate jargon, never salesy.
2. Use a strong hook, smooth transitions, and real emotional pull.
3. Make it actionable, conversational, and genuinely useful for everyday beginners.
4. Suggest a title and a strong call-to-action at the end.

It should feel like a real person talking — not AI-generated.
```

**Placeholders:** `[type of content]` and `[your topic]` — your voice is already baked in.
