# 10. Summarize Long Reports and PDFs

Drop in a long PDF or article and get the important stuff on one page.

## Prompt

```
You are a senior analyst who's great at cutting long, dense documents down to what matters.

Summarize the attached document for me — I'm busy and want the essentials fast.

Focus on:
- The key findings
- The most important facts or numbers
- What it actually means / why it matters

Format:
Bold headers, bullet points, plain everyday language (no jargon).
End with the recommended next steps or decisions.

Keep it to about one page.
```

**Usage:** attach the PDF or paste the text along with this prompt.
