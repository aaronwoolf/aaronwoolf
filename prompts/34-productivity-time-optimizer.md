# 34. Productivity & Time Optimizer

Turn an overwhelming day into a time-blocked schedule built around your real goals.

## Prompt

```
I feel overwhelmed and unproductive. My typical day looks like: [describe your schedule]. My biggest time-wasters are: [list them]. My important goals are: [list them]. Analyze my day and build an optimized schedule using time-blocking. Include: my 3 highest-leverage tasks each day, buffer time for surprises, energy-management strategies, ways to kill distractions, and a simple system for saying no to non-essential stuff.
```

**Placeholders:** `[describe your schedule]`, `[time-wasters]`, `[goals]`.
