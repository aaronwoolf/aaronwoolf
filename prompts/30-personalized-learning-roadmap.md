# 30. Personalized Learning Roadmap

A week-by-week plan to go from total beginner to advanced at any skill.

## Prompt

```
I want to learn [the skill or topic you want — e.g. making short-form videos] from complete beginner to advanced. Create a 12-week learning roadmap broken down week by week. Include: specific free resources (courses, books, videos), daily practice exercises, weekly projects to build a portfolio, common mistakes to avoid, and milestones to track my progress. Tailor it to someone who can put in 1-2 hours a day and is starting from scratch.
```

**Placeholder:** `[the skill or topic you want]` — what you're learning.
