# 31. Relationship Conflict Resolver

Work through a conflict calmly — see their side, and get three ways to talk it out.

## Prompt

```
I'm having a conflict with [who — friend / partner / family member] about [the issue]. Here's the situation: [describe what's going on]. Help me understand their perspective, point out what I might be missing, and craft a calm, non-confrontational way to say how I feel using "I" statements. Then give me 3 ways to resolve it: one assertive, one compromising, and one focused on understanding their needs first.
```

**Placeholders:** `[who]`, `[the issue]`, `[describe what's going on]` — your private details; fill them in.
