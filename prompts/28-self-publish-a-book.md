# 28. Self-Publish a Book on Amazon

Go from niche to a finished first chapter — ideas, a full outline, and chapter 1 written for you.

## Prompt

```
I want to publish a non-fiction book on Amazon in the [your book topic — e.g. making money with AI for beginners] niche.

1. Give me 10 specific book ideas with clear, search-friendly titles that solve a real problem.
2. For my pick, generate a complete chapter-by-chapter outline.
3. For chapter 1, write the full text — clear, conversational, and organized around a problem-and-solution structure, in a casual and human voice (no jargon, not salesy).

Interview me one question at a time until you have enough to give me a GREAT answer.
```

**Placeholder:** `[your book topic]` — pre-seeded with your niche; change it for any book subject.
