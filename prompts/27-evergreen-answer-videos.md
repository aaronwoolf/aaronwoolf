# 27. Evergreen Answer Videos

Build a library of timeless videos that answer the questions people in your niche search over and over. The strategy from the slide: **record 5, upload 5, then do another 5 — you're not chasing virality, you're drilling wells.**

> Note: the source slide showed only this strategy, not the exact Claude prompt. The prompt below is reconstructed to match the same style and goal.

## Prompt

```
I'm building a YouTube channel of evergreen "answer" videos in [YOUR DOMAIN] — short videos that answer the questions people search again and again, year after year.

Give me 20 evergreen video ideas where:
1. The question has steady, long-term search demand (not a passing trend).
2. The answer won't go out of date quickly.
3. There's room to naturally recommend a tool, product, or affiliate offer.

For each idea give me the exact title (phrased the way people actually search), the core question it answers, and a one-line outline of how to answer it clearly.
```

**Placeholder:** `[YOUR DOMAIN]` — your niche or topic area.
