# 33. Health & Fitness Transformation Plan

An 8-week plan built around your goal, your stats, and where you actually train.

## Prompt

```
I want to [lose weight / build muscle / boost energy]. My stats: [age, weight, height, activity level, any dietary restrictions]. Build me a complete 8-week plan with: a daily meal plan with simple recipes and macros, a workout routine I can do [at home / at the gym] for my level, sleep tips, stress-management techniques, and weekly check-in questions that track progress beyond just the scale.
```

**Placeholders:** `[your goal]`, `[your stats]`, `[at home / at the gym]`.

> Not medical advice — check with a doctor before starting a new diet or workout.
