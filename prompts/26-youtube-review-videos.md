# 26. Review Videos on YouTube

Spin up a review channel around products people are already searching to buy, so each video targets existing demand and an affiliate/partnership angle.

## Prompt

```
I'm building a YouTube channel that reviews products in [YOUR DOMAIN] — products people are already actively searching to buy.

Give me 20 review video ideas where:
1. The product has clear existing search demand.
2. Viewers are in the buying phase.
3. There's an affiliate or partnership opportunity.

For each idea give me the exact title, the search intent behind it, and the angle that builds trust without being salesy.
```

**Placeholder:** `[YOUR DOMAIN]` — your niche or product category (e.g. home fitness, budget cameras, productivity software).
