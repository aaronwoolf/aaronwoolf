# 28. Self-Publish a Book on Amazon

Go from niche to finished first chapter — book ideas, a full outline, and a written chapter 1 — with Claude interviewing you to fill in what it needs.

## Prompt

```
I want to publish a non-fiction book on Amazon in the [YOUR TOPIC] niche.

1. Give me 10 specific book ideas with clear, search-friendly titles that solve a defined problem.
2. For my pick, generate a complete chapter-by-chapter outline.
3. For chapter 1, write the full text — clear, conversational, organized around a problem-and-solution structure.

Interview me until you have enough information to give me a GREAT answer.
```

**Placeholder:** `[YOUR TOPIC]` — the subject/niche of the book. Tip: paste it into Claude, then answer its interview questions before it writes chapter 1.
